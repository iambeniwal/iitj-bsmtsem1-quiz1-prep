Quiz 1 Prep

Revision sheets for all six Semester 1 courses, each with topic notes drawn from the lectures and a timed question drill. Sorted by whichever quiz is next.

How the quizzes are marked. Each is Quiz 1, worth 15% of that course's grade, on the LMS with dual-device proctoring and a 15-minute buffer before the start time. Across the semester only your best 2 of 3 quiz scores count per course — a missed attempt is a zero, so an early quiz is cheap insurance for the later ones.

Student-made study aids, not official IIT Jodhpur or Masai School course material. Timings from the LMS exam schedule read on 26 September 2026 — always confirm against the LMS, which is authoritative.
