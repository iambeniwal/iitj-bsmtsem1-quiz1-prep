Quiz 1 Prep

Revision sheets for all six Semester 1 courses, each with topic notes drawn from the lectures and a timed question drill. Sorted by whichever quiz is next.

How the quizzes are marked. Every quiz is on the LMS with dual-device proctoring and a 15-minute join buffer, and every course counts only your best 2 of 3 scores — a missed attempt is a zero. All six use negative marking: +1 for a correct answer, −0.25 for a wrong one, 0 if you leave it blank. That makes a blind guess worth almost nothing (+0.06) but an educated one clearly worthwhile (+0.17 after ruling out a single option). Weighting is not uniform: Foundations of Computing is 15% a quiz; the other five are 20%.
